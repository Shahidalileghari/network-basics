# Lab 01 — Inspect Your Network

Linux: `ip addr`, `ip route`, `cat /etc/resolv.conf`, `ping -c 4 127.0.0.1`.

Windows: `ipconfig /all`, `route print`, `nslookup example.com`, `ping 127.0.0.1`.

macOS: `ifconfig`, `netstat -rn`, `scutil --dns`, `ping -c 4 127.0.0.1`.

Record active interface, address/prefix, default gateway, DNS resolver, and one observation. Do not publish sensitive network details.

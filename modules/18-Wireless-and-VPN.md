# Wireless, VPNs, and Remote Access

Wi-Fi performance is affected by interference, channel overlap, distance, walls, and client capabilities.

A VPN creates an authenticated encrypted tunnel between endpoints or networks. It changes the path and trust boundary; it does not make a device inherently safe or anonymous.

Site-to-site VPNs connect networks; remote-access VPNs connect individual devices. Split tunneling routes selected traffic through the tunnel; full tunneling routes most or all traffic through it.

# Review and Interview Preparation

Be ready to explain what happens after entering a URL; DNS resolution; TCP vs UDP; TCP handshake; MAC vs IP; CIDR and subnet masks; default gateways; switch vs router; HTTPS and certificate validation; diagnosing DNS failures; NAT vs firewall; and how a mobile app securely calls a backend API.

For every answer, explain the problem, mechanism, trade-offs, and how you would observe it.
